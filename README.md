## 🎯 What is this?

A single, self-contained, dark-themed interactive web page that takes you from **"I last watched when Drogba and Fàbregas were kings"** all the way to **"Mbappé vs Haaland at World Cup 2026."**

No frameworks. No build step. No dependencies. Just open `index.html` and everything works.

---

## 📖 What's inside

| Section | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| 🏆 **The Legends Era** | 30 icons from your era — Messi, Ronaldo, Xavi, Iniesta, Kaká, Drogba, Buffon, Neuer, Crouch and more, grouped by position |
| 📅 **The Big Story (2012→2026)** | A 17-point timeline of every defining moment: Agüero's 93:20, Leicester 5000/1, Neymar's €222m move, Messi's 2022 World Cup, and more |
| 🏟️ **Club Dynasties** | Rise & fall of ~9 clubs per league across all 5 major leagues |
| ⚔️ **Old Legends vs New Stars** | 12 side-by-side comparisons that "translate" today's stars into players you already know |
| 🌟 **Who To Know for 2026** | 18 current superstars with social-media reach and signature traits |
| 📊 **League Histories** | Season-by-season title winners + detailed events for the PL, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1 & Champions League |
| 🌍 **The Nations** | Year-by-year journeys for 16 major national teams (England, France, Argentina, Spain, Brazil, Germany & more) |
| 🗺️ **WC 2026 Groups** | All 12 groups rated by difficulty (🔥 Group of Death → Medium) |
| 👕 **All Squads** | Key players for every team with "what to watch" notes |
| 🗓️ **Full Fixtures** | All 104 matches with kick-off times in ET / UK / Pakistan |

---

## ✨ Features

- **🌑 Slick dark UI** with a green accent, smooth fade-in animations and a sticky nav
- **🔀 Tabbed everything** — switch between leagues, nations, squads and fixtures instantly
- **📱 Fully responsive** — looks great on mobile and desktop
- **⚡ Zero dependencies** — one HTML file, fonts from Google, nothing else
- **🎨 Colour-coded** title-winner grids so you can see dynasties at a glance

---

## 🛠️ Tech

- Pure **HTML + CSS + vanilla JavaScript** (no React, no build tools)
- Fonts: [Anton](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Anton) + [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)
- `IntersectionObserver` for scroll animations
- ~6,000 lines, all in one `index.html`
