Where You Left Off · 2008–2014
THE LEGENDS ERA

You stopped watching around 2010–2012. This was the golden generation — the players ruling world football when you left, and what happened to each of them.

⚽ THE TWO KINGS

Barcelona · Argentina
Lionel Messi
When you left, he was just becoming the best on earth. He then won the Ballon d'Or 8 times (no one else has more than 5), scored 91 goals in 2012 alone (a calendar-year record), won the 2022 World Cup, and is STILL playing in the 2026 WC. The greatest of all time. Now at Inter Miami.
Real Madrid · Portugal
Cristiano Ronaldo
Messi's eternal rival. Won 5 Ballon d'Ors, 5 Champions Leagues, scored 450 goals for Real Madrid. Then Juventus, then Saudi Arabia. 143 international goals — a world record. 660M Instagram. Also still at the 2026 WC at age 41. Unfathomable longevity.

🎩 THE MIDFIELD MAESTROS

Barcelona · Spain
Xavi Hernández
The greatest passing midfielder ever. The brain of Barcelona's tiki-taka and Spain's 2008–2012 dominance (Euro, WC, Euro). Left for Qatar in 2015, returned as Barca manager 2021–24. Built the team around Pedri and Lamine Yamal.
Barcelona · Spain
Andrés Iniesta
Scored Spain's WORLD CUP WINNING goal in 2010. The quiet genius. Glided past players like they weren't there. Retired 2024 after Japan and UAE spells. Loved by every football fan on earth — even rival ones.
Chelsea · England
Frank Lampard
England's greatest goalscoring midfielder — 211 PL goals from midfield. Engine, leadership, late runs into the box. Retired 2017, managed Chelsea + Everton. Compare him now to Bellingham.
Liverpool · England
Steven Gerrard
Liverpool's heart and soul. Single-handedly dragged them to the 2005 UCL comeback (the "Miracle of Istanbul"). Never won the league — the famous slip in 2014 cost them. Managed Aston Villa + Rangers. A one-club English legend.
Man United · England
Paul Scholes
The most underrated midfielder in PL history. Xavi and Zidane both called him the best of his generation. Ginger genius. Retired 2011, un-retired 2012, retired again 2013. Famously hated doing interviews.
Real Madrid · Spain
Xabi Alonso
The most elegant deep-lying playmaker. Liverpool, Real Madrid, Bayern. Retired 2017. NOW he's the hottest manager in the world — won the Bundesliga UNBEATEN with Bayer Leverkusen in 2024. A future Real Madrid boss.

🇧🇷 THE BRAZILIAN FLAIR

Milan/Real Madrid · Brazil
Kaká
The last Brazilian (before Vinícius/Neymar) to win the Ballon d'Or — in 2007. Glided across the pitch at AC Milan, won the 2007 UCL. Real Madrid record signing in 2009 (€67m). Injuries slowed him but at his peak — sublime. Retired 2017. A gentleman of the game.
Santos→Barcelona · Brazil
Neymar Jr
When you left he was the teenage sensation at Santos. Joined Barcelona 2013 (the BBC trio with Messi + Suárez, won the 2015 treble). Then the €222m world-record move to PSG in 2017. Injuries derailed his peak. Now at Santos again, and BACK in Brazil's 2026 WC squad. 226M Instagram.
Inter/Barcelona · Brazil
Ronaldinho
Just before your era but the man who made football JOY. The toothy smile, the no-look passes, the impossible flicks. Made a young Messi look up to him at Barcelona. Won the 2005 Ballon d'Or. The most purely entertaining player ever. Retired 2018.
Real Madrid · Brazil
Marcelo
The attacking left-back who won FIVE Champions Leagues with Real Madrid. Curly hair, samba dribbles, Roberto Carlos's heir. One of the most decorated full-backs in history. Retired 2024.

🦁 THE STRIKERS & FORWARDS

Chelsea · Ivory Coast
Didier Drogba
The ultimate big-game striker. Scored the 88th-minute header AND the winning penalty in the 2012 UCL Final. Won 4 PL titles. Unplayable when it mattered most. Retired 2018 — still Chelsea's greatest-ever striker.
Man United → LA → England
Wayne Rooney
Man Utd's all-time top scorer (253 goals). England's talisman. The bicycle-kick vs City (2011) is one of the PL's greatest goals. Peaked when you were watching. Retired 2021, managed Derby, Birmingham, Plymouth.
Stoke/Spurs/Liverpool · England
Peter Crouch
The 6'7" cult hero. Did the famous "robot" celebration. Surprisingly skilful for a giant — great touch, scored bicycle kicks. 22 England goals. Beloved across the country. Now a top podcaster + TV personality. Proof football needs characters as well as superstars.
Barcelona/Liverpool · Uruguay
Luis Suárez
The genius and the villain. The 2010 handball that knocked out Ghana. The bites (yes, multiple). But also one of the best strikers ever — part of Barca's BBC, won everything. Remarkably, STILL at the 2026 WC for his farewell. You'll see him.
Man City/Atlético · Argentina
Sergio Agüero
"AGÜEROOOO!" — scored the most dramatic goal in PL history: the 93:20 winner that won City their first title in 2012. City's all-time top scorer. Pure striker instinct. Heart condition forced retirement in 2021. A legend.
Sweden · multiple clubs
Zlatan Ibrahimović
The most arrogant, most quotable, most spectacular striker of the era. Ajax, Juve, Inter, Barca, Milan, PSG, Man United, LA Galaxy, Milan again. Scored insane karate-kick goals into his 40s. "I am Zlatan." Retired 2023. An icon.

🧤 THE GREAT GOALKEEPERS

Juventus · Italy
Gianluigi Buffon
Widely considered the greatest goalkeeper ever. Won the 2006 World Cup. Played top-level football until age 45 (!). 176 caps for Italy. The benchmark every keeper is measured against. Pure shot-stopping perfection + incredible longevity.
Real Madrid · Spain
Iker Casillas
"San Iker" — Saint Iker. Won the World Cup, 2 Euros, 3 Champions Leagues. Captain of Spain's golden era. Lightning reflexes. The save vs Robben in the 2010 WC Final basically won Spain the trophy. Survived a heart attack in 2019.
Bayern Munich · Germany
Manuel Neuer
REVOLUTIONISED goalkeeping. Invented the "sweeper-keeper" — playing 30 yards off his line like an extra defender. Won the 2014 World Cup. Still good enough that he came OUT OF RETIREMENT to play the 2026 WC. The most complete keeper ever.
Chelsea/Arsenal · Czech Rep
Petr Čech
202 Premier League clean sheets — the all-time record. Wore that protective head-guard after a fractured skull in 2006 and kept playing brilliantly for a decade. The rock behind Chelsea's 2012 UCL win. Now a hockey goalie (seriously).

Barcelona · Spain

The most decorated and most aggressive defender ever. Scored the 93rd-minute header that won the 2014 UCL ("La Décima"). Most red cards in football history but also clutch beyond belief. Real Madrid + Spain legend.

THE BIG STORY
2012 → 2026

Every major moment in football's last 14 years, top to bottom. The transfers, the shocks, the dynasties, the dramas.

🏆
2012

Sergio Agüero's 93:20 + Chelsea Win the Champions League

TWO iconic 2012 moments. First, Agüero scored in the 93rd minute on the final day to win Man City their first title in 44 years — the most dramatic finish in PL history. Then Chelsea won the UCL on penalties in Munich, Drogba scoring the equaliser and winner. Spain also won Euro 2012, completing an unprecedented Euro-WC-Euro treble.

Agüerooooo
🔚
2013

Sir Alex Ferguson Retires — End of an Empire

After 26 years and 13 league titles, the most successful manager in British history retired from Man United. The club has NEVER recovered — they haven't won the league since. Also in 2013: Neymar joined Barcelona, forming the BBC trio with Messi and Suárez. And Bayern won the treble under Heynckes.

Fergie out
⭐
2014 World Cup

Germany Destroy Brazil 7–1. James Lights Up the Tournament.

The most shocking result in WC history — Brazil, hosting, lost their semi-final 7–1 (5–0 in 29 minutes). The nation wept. Germany won the final vs Argentina (Messi so close again). James Rodríguez (Colombia) won the Golden Boot with stunning goals and earned a Real Madrid move. Also: Suárez bit someone. Again.

The Mineirazo
👑
2014–2018

Real Madrid Win 4 Champions Leagues in 5 Years

An unprecedented dynasty. "La Décima" (10th) in 2014, then THREE IN A ROW (2016, 2017, 2018) under Zidane — no club had done that in the modern era. Ronaldo was unstoppable in Europe. The most dominant Champions League run ever seen.

Madrid's European empire
🦊
2015–2016

Leicester City Win the Premier League (5000/1)

The greatest sporting upset of all time. A team of journeymen — Jamie Vardy (ex-factory worker), Mahrez, Kanté — won the league at 5000/1 odds. Nobody believed it. Manager Ranieri was sacked nine months later (football is cruel). Still the most beautiful story in modern sport.

Greatest upset ever
💰
2017

Neymar to PSG for €222m — The Market Breaks Forever

PSG (Qatar-backed) paid €222m for Neymar — STILL the world record. Every fee instantly doubled. Mbappé joined PSG the same summer. Barcelona spent the Neymar money on Coutinho (€160m flop) and Dembélé. Football economics changed permanently that day.

Transfer market explodes
🇷🇺
2018 World Cup

France Win. Mbappé Arrives. Croatia & England Shock.

France won in Russia — 19-year-old Mbappé became the first teenager since Pelé to score in a WC Final. England reached their first semi since 1990 ("It's Coming Home" mania). Croatia (Modrić, who won the Ballon d'Or that year) reached the final from a nation of just 4 million. Germany crashed out in the GROUP STAGE — humiliating for the holders.

Mbappé announced
👑
2019

Liverpool's Greatest Comeback + Klopp's Revolution

Klopp's Liverpool came back from 3–0 down to beat Barcelona 4–0 (one of football's greatest nights), then won the UCL. In 2019/20 they won their FIRST league title in 30 years. Salah, Mané, Firmino up front; Van Dijk and Alisson at the back. A team for the ages.

Liverpool reborn
😷
2020

Covid Stops Football. Empty Stadiums. The Weird Year.

Football paused for 3 months then resumed behind closed doors — ghost games with fake crowd noise. Euro 2020 was delayed a year. Surreal. Also: the European Super League was attempted in 2021 by 12 elite clubs, then collapsed within 48 hours after fan protests. A near-disaster for the sport's soul.

The pandemic + Super League fiasco
🇮🇹
Euro 2020 (played 2021)

Italy Win — England's Wembley Penalty Heartbreak

Italy beat England on penalties at Wembley. Saka, Rashford and Sancho missed — then suffered horrific racist abuse online (a dark moment that sparked national reckoning). Donnarumma was the hero. England's "coming home" dream died at the final hurdle again.

Penalty trauma again
🌟
2021–2023

Pep's Man City Dominate + Win the Treble

Guardiola built the most dominant English team ever — 4 titles in 5 years. Signed Haaland in 2022, who scored 36 PL goals in his debut season (a record). City won the TREBLE in 2023 (PL + FA Cup + Champions League) — finally conquering Europe. The new gold standard.

City's treble
😢
2021

Messi Leaves Barcelona in Tears

Unthinkable. Barcelona were so financially broke (€1.3bn debt) they couldn't afford to renew Messi's contract. He cried at the press conference. Joined PSG, then Inter Miami in 2023 (sparking an MLS boom). The end of a 21-year era. Barcelona's decline was complete.

End of an era
⭐
2022 World Cup — Qatar

MESSI WINS IT. The Greatest Final Ever Played.

Argentina 2–0 up. Then Mbappé scored a hat-trick from the 80th minute to make it 3–3 after extra time. Penalties. Messi vs Mbappé. Argentina won. Every neutral cried with joy — Messi's 5th and final attempt finally succeeded, cementing him as the GOAT. PLUS: Morocco became Africa's first-ever semi-finalists; Japan beat Germany AND Spain; Saudi Arabia beat Argentina. The best WC ever.

Greatest final in history
🇸🇦
2023

Saudi Arabia Buys World Football

Ronaldo joined Al Nassr in January 2023, opening the floodgates. Then Benzema, Mané, Neymar, Henderson, Firmino, Kanté, Brozović. The Saudi Pro League suddenly had more stars than Serie A. Funded by the Saudi state's PIF. Sportswashing debate raged. Newcastle (also Saudi-owned since 2021) qualified for the UCL.

Petrodollar era
🔴
2023–2024

Man United Collapse · Chelsea Spend £1bn · Arsenal Near-Miss

United spent £1.5bn on mostly flops, sacked Ten Hag, brought in Amorim — still chaos. Chelsea (new owner Boehly) spent over £1bn in two years and stayed mediocre. Arsenal (Arteta) finished 2nd two years running but STILL haven't won the league since 2004 — England's longest-running tragedy. Klopp shockingly left Liverpool.

PL power shifts
🌟
2024

Lamine Yamal (16) Wins Euro 2024 · Mbappé Joins Real Madrid

Spain won Euro 2024 in Germany. 16-year-old Lamine Yamal — playing on his birthday in the semi — scored a screamer vs France. The next Messi. Rodri won the Ballon d'Or. Meanwhile Mbappé FINALLY joined Real Madrid (on a free transfer!) after years of speculation, leaving PSG. And Xabi Alonso's Leverkusen won the Bundesliga unbeaten — a first.

New generation takes over
💷
2025

Wirtz to Liverpool £116m · Slot's Title · Transfer Insanity

New Liverpool boss Arne Slot won the league in his debut season (2024/25), then spent £116m on German sensation Wirtz — a British record. Transfer fees reached absurd new heights across Europe. The gap between the super-rich clubs and everyone else became a chasm. Football's money era hit warp speed.

Records shattered

The Changing of the Guard
CLUB DYNASTIES
RISE & FALL

Every major club's journey from 2012 to 2026 across Europe's top 5 leagues. Who rose, who fell, who got bought, who imploded.

🔵 Manchester City
DOMINANT RISE
Abu Dhabi-owned since 2008. Pep Guardiola arrived 2016 and built the greatest English team ever. 8 PL titles since 2012, the 2023 treble. Haaland breaks every record. BUT: facing 100+ financial fair play charges that could shake the club. The defining team of the era — for now.
🔴 Liverpool
SPECTACULAR REVIVAL
Were trophyless for years when you left. Klopp arrived 2015, built a pressing machine — UCL 2019, PL 2020 (first in 30 years). Klopp left 2024; Slot won the title in 2024/25. Signed Wirtz for £116m. Salah is now their all-time great. Back at the very top.
🔴 Manchester United
CATASTROPHIC DECLINE
Ferguson retired 2013 → total collapse. Moyes, Van Gaal, Mourinho, Solskjaer, Ten Hag, Amorim — all failed. Spent £1.5bn+ on flops (Antony €95m, Sancho, Maguire). Now part-owned by Sir Jim Ratcliffe (INEOS). 20+ years of mismanagement. England's saddest fall from grace.
🔵 Chelsea
OWNERSHIP CHAOS
Abramovich sanctioned in 2022 (Russia/Ukraine), sold to Boehly for £4.25bn. Then spent £1bn+ in two years — records shattered, results mediocre. Fired Tuchel, Potter, Lampard (again), Pochettino, Maresca. Won the Conference League + Club World Cup but no consistency. Chaos club.
🔴 Arsenal
NEAR-MISS REVIVAL
Wenger left 2018 after a long decline. Arteta (ex-City coach) rebuilt brilliantly — Saka, Ødegaard, Rice, now Gyökeres. Finished 2nd THREE times recently but STILL no league title since 2004. The 20-year wait is England's longest-running soap opera. So close, yet…
⚫ Newcastle United
SAUDI TAKEOVER
Bought by Saudi PIF in 2021 for £305m, ending the miserable Mike Ashley era. Immediate investment. Qualified for the UCL in 2023, won the League Cup in 2025 (first major trophy in 70 years!). Isak was their star. Eddie Howe building a genuine force.
⚪ Tottenham Hotspur
NEARLY MEN → TROPHY
UCL Final 2019 (lost). Years of "Spursy" near-misses and no trophies. Harry Kane left for Bayern in 2023 chasing silverware. Then in 2025 Spurs FINALLY won the Europa League — their first trophy in 17 years. Son Heung-min was their hero before leaving. Relief at last.
🟣 Aston Villa
REMARKABLE REVIVAL
Nearly bankrupt, relegated in 2016. New American owners + Unai Emery (2022) transformed them into UCL qualifiers. Signed Emi Martínez, Tielemans, Rashford. Now a regular European side. One of the decade's best revival stories.
🍒 Bournemouth / Brighton / Brentford
THE SMART CLUBS
The "moneyball" revolution. Brighton (owner Tony Bloom uses data/analytics) discovered Caicedo, Mac Allister, then sold them for £100m+ each. Brentford too. These small clubs out-think the giants by buying cheap and selling high. Changed how football recruits.
🦊 Leicester City
5000/1 GLORY → CHAOS
Won the PL in 2016 (greatest underdog story ever). FA Cup in 2021. Then financial mismanagement: relegated 2023, promoted 2024, relegated again. A cautionary tale of the modern game. Mahrez (now at the WC with Algeria) was their star.
⚪ Real Madrid
THE EUROPEAN EMPIRE
Won the UCL 6 times from 2014–2024 — including three in a row under Zidane. Ancelotti returned and won everything again. Signed Bellingham (2023) and Mbappé (2024, on a free!). The most successful club of the era by a distance. The galáctico machine never stops.
🔵🔴 Barcelona
COLLAPSE → REBUILD
The BBC era was glorious (2014–17 treble). Then financial implosion — €1.3bn debt, couldn't afford to keep Messi (he left crying in 2021). Years of selling players and "economic levers". But now Lamine Yamal (18) and Pedri give them genuine hope again. A sleeping giant waking up.
🔴⚪ Atlético Madrid
SIMEONE'S FORTRESS
Diego Simeone has managed them since 2011 — incredible loyalty. 2 La Liga titles (2014, 2021), 2 UCL Finals (both lost, agonisingly, to Real Madrid). Defensive, intense, never-say-die. Griezmann is their legend. The third force in Spain that refuses to fade.
🔴⚪ Sevilla
EUROPA LEAGUE KINGS
Won the Europa League a record 7 times. Master of the buy-low-sell-high model — developed Koundé, Navas, Ocampos, Jordán. The smartest club below the big three. Though recently struggling financially.
🦇 Valencia / 🟢 Real Betis
FADED GIANTS
Valencia were a UCL regular in the 2000s but ownership problems (Peter Lim) caused a long decline. Betis (Seville's other club) became the romantic mid-table darlings — Isco revived his career there. Spanish football below the big three has struggled financially.
🔴⚪ Bayern Munich
DECADE OF DOMINANCE
Won the Bundesliga ELEVEN times in a row (2013–2023) — total monopoly. UCL in 2013 and 2020 (treble). Signed the best of everyone — Lewandowski (then sold to Barca 2022), now Harry Kane (yes, England's Kane plays here). Rebuilding around Musiala. Still Germany's superpower.
🔴⚫ Bayer Leverkusen
"NEVERKUSEN" NO MORE
For decades the club that always finished 2nd ("Neverkusen"). Then in 2023/24 they won the Bundesliga UNBEATEN — the first ever unbeaten season in German history. The manager? Xabi Alonso, the player you remember. Now the most coveted coach in the world.
🟡⚫ Borussia Dortmund
THE TALENT FACTORY
The club that develops and sells the world's best: Haaland (→ City), Sancho (→ United), Bellingham (→ Real Madrid), Dembélé (→ Barca), Pulisic. Reached the UCL Final in 2024 (lost to Real Madrid). Always exciting, rarely wins the biggest prizes. The yellow wall is the best atmosphere in Germany.
⚫⚪ RB Leipzig
RED BULL'S PROJECT
Red Bull built this club from the 4th tier (2009) to UCL football in a decade. Many fans hate the artificial model (no traditional fan culture). But undeniably effective — Werner, Nkunku, Olmo all developed here. The future of "corporate" football, for better or worse.
⚫⚪ Juventus
DOMINANCE → SCANDAL
Won Serie A 9 times in a row (2012–2020). Signed Ronaldo in 2018. But never won the UCL. Then a financial scandal ("plusvalenza" — fake transfer values) led to points deductions, board resignations, and UEFA bans. A proud club humbled. Now slowly rebuilding.
🔵⚫ Inter Milan
SERIE A's COMEBACK KINGS
Struggled post-2010 treble. Then Chinese owners (Suning) invested. Won Serie A in 2021 and 2024. Reached the UCL Final in 2023 and 2025 (lost both). Now have Lautaro Martínez (WC), Çalhanoğlu, Thuram. Italy's strongest club again.
🔴⚫ AC Milan
HISTORIC REVIVAL
Were in mid-table mediocrity when you left, even outside Europe. Rebuilt patiently. Won Serie A in 2022 (first since 2011). American owners (RedBird). Now feature Pulisic (USA), Leão, Modrić (yes, joined in 2025!), Giménez. The Rossoneri are relevant again.
🔵 Napoli
MARADONA'S CLUB WINS AGAIN
Won their first Serie A since Maradona's 1990 era — in 2023, with Osimhen and Kvaratskhelia unstoppable. Then won it AGAIN in 2025 under Antonio Conte. Now have De Bruyne AND Lukaku from Belgium's golden generation. A genuine powerhouse now.
🟡🔴 Roma / 🔵 Lazio / ⚫🔵 Atalanta
THE CHALLENGERS
Atalanta are the surprise story — from small-town club to UCL regulars and Europa League winners (2024) under Gasperini, playing thrilling attacking football. Roma had a Mourinho era (won the Conference League 2022). Italian football is more competitive than it's been in years.
🔵🔴 PSG
MONEY → FINALLY UCL
Qatar-owned since 2011. Spent billions: Ibrahimović, Neymar (€222m record), Mbappé, Messi. Despite everything, couldn't win the UCL for over a decade. Mbappé left on a free in 2024. Then — rebuilt around youth (Dembélé, Doué, Vitinha) — they FINALLY won the Champions League in 2025. Vindication at last.
🔴⚫ Monaco
YOUTH GLORY
Won Ligue 1 in 2017 and reached the UCL semis with a stunning young team: Mbappé (pre-PSG), Bernardo Silva, Fabinho, Lemar. All sold for huge fees. The classic French model — develop, sell, repeat. A feeder club for Europe's elite.
🔵 Lyon
POWERHOUSE → CHAOS
France's dominant club in the 2000s (7 straight titles). Then American owner John Textor (2022) brought financial disaster — unpaid wages, relegation threats, off-field chaos. One of European football's saddest declines. The women's team, however, remains the best in the world.
⚪🔵 Marseille / 🔴 Lille / 🟣 Toulouse
THE PROUD REST
Marseille has France's most passionate fans (the Vélodrome) but can't match PSG's money. Lille won a shock title in 2021 (beating PSG!) and developed Jonathan David (now at the WC with Canada). Ligue 1 is essentially PSG plus everyone fighting for scraps.

Man United, early days. Step-overs, pace, fearless dribbling before he became a pure striker. The most exciting wide player alive then.

Real Madrid · Brazil. Will beat 3 defenders with one run, then finish in the top corner. The most thrilling winger on earth right now.

Bayern · Germany. INVENTED the modern sweeper-keeper — played like an outfield defender, charged off his line. Revolutionary.

Arsenal · France. Calm, elegant, dominant. Considered one of the best CBs in the world before turning 24. The modern leader at the back.

City hit 100 points. Pep's "Centurions" — the first team to reach 100 points in a PL season. 32 wins, +79 goal difference, both records. Salah arrived at Liverpool and scored 32 league goals (a 38-game record). De Bruyne was unplayable.
2018/19
The greatest title race. City win with 98 points; Liverpool finish 2nd with 97 (most points ever for a non-champion). City won their last 14 games straight. Agüero, Sterling, Salah, Mané all electric. Decided on the final day.
2019/20
Liverpool end 30 years of hurt. Klopp's side won the title by 18 points, clinched in June after the Covid "Project Restart" in empty stadiums. They were 25 points clear at one stage. Mané, Salah, Firmino + Van Dijk + Alisson — a team for the ages.
2020/21
City reclaim it + Super League fiasco. City won comfortably. But the season's defining moment was off-pitch: in April, 12 elite clubs (6 English) tried to form a breakaway European Super League — and it collapsed in 48 hours after furious fan protests. A near-death moment for the sport's structure.
2021/22
City's final-day comeback + Abramovich sanctioned. City were 2–0 down to Villa on the last day, then scored 3 in 5 minutes to pip Liverpool by a point. Earlier, Roman Abramovich was sanctioned over Russia's invasion of Ukraine — Chelsea were sold to Todd Boehly for £4.25bn.
2022/23
The Treble + Haaland's record. Haaland scored 36 league goals in his DEBUT season (a PL record), and City won the Premier League, FA Cup AND Champions League — only the second English club ever to do the Treble after United in 1999. Arsenal led for months then collapsed.
2023/24
Four in a row — a first. City became the first club EVER to win four consecutive English top-flight titles. Pipped Arsenal by 2 points again. Foden was Player of the Season. Meanwhile City's 100+ financial charges loomed over everything.
2024/25
Slot's instant title. Klopp left; Arne Slot replaced him and won the league at the first attempt with games to spare. Salah had a monster season (29 goals, Player of the Year) before signing a new deal. City's dynasty finally cracked.
